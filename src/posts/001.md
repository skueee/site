Heyyy!

I'm **skue**, the webmaster of this brand new (at the time of writing) website.

Of course, there is still a loooot of things missing, but I'm very happy with the current result!

## But why doing a website?

If you're like me, you probably really like looking at other devs page.

After staring at pages like [temidara.dev](https://temidara.dev/) or [sameerasw.com](https://sameerasw.com/), I knew I needed my own site, so I (well, with [Stardance](https://stardance.hackclub.com)'s money) bought a domain and started making a website!

I think it's pretty nice to have a place ot post thoughts, share what I like, and I have some plans to do just that!

## How did you do that?

I started by designing some prototype on [Figma](https://figma.com/), then I started developping with SvelteKit.

I used some advices on the [IndieWeb Wiki](https://indieweb.org/) and on [indiewebify.me](https://indiewebify.me/) to implement things like webmention, h-card... I still need to do a lot tho!
